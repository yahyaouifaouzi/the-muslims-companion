# Privacy Policy — Rafiq (رفيق المسلم)

**Last updated:** 2026-10-14

Rafiq is an offline Muslim companion app. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with your data.

## Data the app collects

**None.** Rafiq does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
There is:

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics or crash reporting.
- No advertising or ad identifiers.
- No server-side data storage.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only:

- Favorites (bookmarked adhkar/duas/hadiths).
- Repetition-counter progress and any target you set (progress resets
  daily; targets persist).
- The last entry you opened, used only for the "continue where you left
  off" card on the home screen (you can dismiss it).
- Reminder toggles and times (if you enable the optional morning or
  evening reminder).
- Cached content, reading font size, and light/dark appearance choice.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (optional): used only if you enable the morning or
  evening reminder. You can disable either at any time in Settings.
- **Clipboard**: used only when you tap "copy" or when the share sheet is
  unavailable (desktop fallback). Nothing is copied automatically.
- **Network**: used only to check for content updates if the developer has
  configured a content base URL. The bundled content works fully offline and
  no network access is required for core features.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not target children specifically.

## Changes

Any future change to this policy will be reflected in this file with an
updated date. The app itself will not change its data behavior without a
corresponding policy update.

## Contact

Questions or content corrections: open an issue at
https://github.com/ideas2d/TheMuslimsCompanion, or use the in-app
"عن المحتوى" screen (Settings) to send a report email.
